Catalog disagreements kept in the proof:

- 003's energy 6/8 main is once per physical card. The energy-20 active is once per printed name.
- 017's +2 is the catalog amount. The surviving sentence resolves on play, not as a separate once-per-turn main.
- 068 counts raids during the current turn, with no extra own-turn gate. Discard 1 and the energy-line location come from the catalog.
- 070 does not open chain Raid. TTG-1-033 remains unregistered, so no card named exactly グレン is a host target yet.

Chain Raid onto a card that itself has raidName stays illegal.
